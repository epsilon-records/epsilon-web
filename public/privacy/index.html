Privacy

Privacy notice

epsilonrecords.com is a static website. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no forms and no shop, and it sets no cookies. This notice explains the little personal data that is still processed when you visit, and what happens when you email us.

Effective 9 November 2026

01

Who is responsible

The controller for this website is Epsilon Records, Nieuwe Uilenburgerstraat 21C, 1011LM Amsterdam.

For anything about your personal data, write to hello@epsilonrecords.com.

This notice covers epsilonrecords.com only. ERMS, AudioKit and Epsilon Audio are separate sites and services with their own privacy information.

02

When you visit the site

Hosting — Netlify

The site is hosted by Netlify, Inc. (United States) and delivered through its global network. To send you a page, Netlify's servers necessarily receive your IP address and standard request details: the address requested, date and time, browser user agent and referring page. We do not run our own server, keep our own access logs, or combine this data with anything else. Netlify processes it as our hosting provider, under its own terms and retention practices.

Fonts — Google Fonts

Every page loads the IBM Plex typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, operated by Google LLC, United States). Your browser requests the font files directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address, user agent and the address of the page that asked for the fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google's Fonts privacy FAQ.

Analytics — Cloudflare Web Analytics

When enabled, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate page traffic and performance. Its beacon receives page and referring URL information and browser performance measurements. Cloudflare states that the beacon does not use cookies or browser storage or track people across customer sites. Our integration is limited to selected public content paths on epsilonrecords.com. It skips documents with queries or fragments in the current URL, original navigation URL or referrer, and sensitive or unknown paths; the privacy and not-found pages are untracked. See Cloudflare's RUM explanation.

Domain name — Cloudflare DNS

Cloudflare also provides the DNS for the epsilonrecords.com domain. DNS only translates the domain name into the address of our host; page traffic itself goes to Netlify, not through Cloudflare.

No cookies, forms or accounts

The site sets no cookies and has no forms, logins, comments, newsletter sign-up or embedded third-party players. It does not use advertising or social-media tracking.

03

When you email us

If you write to an address shown on this site, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it only to read and answer you and to handle any follow-up, such as a release, collaboration or partnership you raise.

04

Links to other sites

The site links to services we do not run. Our shop lives on Bandcamp: the old /merch addresses redirect to epsilonrecords.bandcamp.com, and anything you buy there is handled by Bandcamp under its own privacy policy. The same applies to links to Instagram, SoundCloud, YouTube, Twitch, Mixcloud, TikTok, Facebook, Discord and the streaming and industry services named on our pages. Nothing is sent to those services until you follow a link.

05

Legal bases

We rely on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to:

  • deliver the website securely and reliably (hosting and DNS);
  • show the site in its intended typeface (Google Fonts);
  • understand, in aggregate and without cookies, how much the site is used and how fast it loads (Cloudflare Web Analytics);
  • answer messages you send us. Where your message is about entering into an agreement with us, such as a release, we also rely on taking steps at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

You can object to any of this at any time (see below).

06

How long data is kept

  • Request data at Netlify and Google Fonts: we keep none ourselves; the providers keep it under their own retention practices.
  • Analytics: we only see aggregate reports. Cloudflare does not give us individual-level data.
  • Email: this notice does not specify a fixed retention period for correspondence. Retention depends on the enquiry, any resulting relationship and applicable record-keeping obligations.

07

International transfers

Netlify, Google and Cloudflare are based in the United States, so data described above may be processed outside the European Economic Area. See Netlify's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy and Cloudflare's privacy policy for their descriptions of international processing.

08

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, and to correct, erase or restrict it, to receive it in a portable form, or object to our processing it. Write to the privacy contact above. Because the site itself collects so little, in practice this mostly concerns emails you have sent us.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; you may also go to the authority where you live or work.

09

Changes

If what the site collects changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
