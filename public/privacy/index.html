Privacy

Privacy notice

epsilonrecords.com and plugins.epsilonrecords.com (Epsilon Audio) are static websites. They have no accounts, no shop and no cookies of their own. The one form is the newsletter sign-up, which is on both sites. This notice explains the little personal data that is processed when you visit either site, when you subscribe to the newsletter and when you email us.

Effective 10 November 2026

01

Who is responsible

The controller for these websites and the newsletter is Epsilon Records, Nieuwe Uilenburgerstraat 21C, 1011LM Amsterdam.

For anything about your personal data, write to hello@epsilonrecords.com.

This notice covers epsilonrecords.com, plugins.epsilonrecords.com and the newsletter. LabelKit (labelkit.co) and AudioKit are separate products with their own privacy information.

02

When you visit our sites

Hosting — Netlify

Both sites are hosted by Netlify, Inc. (United States) and delivered through its global network. To send you a page, Netlify's servers necessarily receive your IP address and standard request details: the address requested, date and time, browser user agent and referring page. We do not run our own server, keep our own access logs, or combine this data with anything else. Netlify processes it as our hosting provider, under its own terms and retention practices.

Fonts — Google Fonts

Every page on both sites loads the IBM Plex typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com, operated by Google LLC, United States). Your browser requests the font files directly from Google, so Google receives your IP address, user agent and the address of the page that asked for the fonts. Google Fonts does not set cookies. See Google's Fonts privacy FAQ.

Analytics — Cloudflare Web Analytics

When enabled, we use Cloudflare Web Analytics on epsilonrecords.com and plugins.epsilonrecords.com to understand aggregate page traffic and performance. Its beacon receives page and referring URL information and browser performance measurements. Cloudflare states that the beacon does not use cookies or browser storage or track people across customer sites. Our integration is limited to selected public content paths on the two sites. It skips documents with queries or fragments in the current URL, original navigation URL or referrer, and sensitive or unknown paths; the privacy, newsletter confirmation and not-found pages are untracked. See Cloudflare's RUM explanation.

Domain name — Cloudflare DNS

Cloudflare also provides the DNS for the epsilonrecords.com domain. DNS only translates the domain name into the address of our host; page traffic itself goes to Netlify, not through Cloudflare.

Releases and downloads — GitHub

The plugins site asks GitHub (api.github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc., United States) for the list of plugin releases. Your browser makes that request directly, so GitHub receives your IP address and browser details. Download buttons and source links on the plugins site point to GitHub, which handles anything you do there under its own privacy statement.

No cookies or accounts

Neither site's own code sets cookies or stores anything on your device, and neither has accounts, logins or comments. Neither embeds third-party players or uses advertising or social-media tracking. The only form is the newsletter sign-up described below.

03

Newsletter

Both plugins.epsilonrecords.com and epsilonrecords.com have a form to subscribe to an occasional email about new plugins, preset packs and track breakdowns from Epsilon Records artists. Nothing is sent anywhere until you submit the form.

What is collected, and how

The email address you enter. The form sends it straight from your browser to Kit (formerly ConvertKit), the service we use to run the newsletter. Kit also receives your IP address and browser details, as any website does when it receives a request. Kit then emails you a confirmation message, and you are subscribed only after you confirm. Once you do, Kit sends you to a confirmation page on epsilonrecords.com. We use your address only to send you the newsletter.

Kit as processor

Kit processes your email address for us as our email provider and also under its own privacy policy. Kit states that it may transfer personal data to the United States and elsewhere using the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (Kit's GDPR page).

Unsubscribing

Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can also write to hello@epsilonrecords.com and ask us to remove you.

04

When you email us

If you write to an address shown on this site, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it only to read and answer you and to handle any follow-up, such as a release, collaboration or partnership you raise.

05

Links to other sites

The site links to services we do not run. Our shop lives on Bandcamp: the old /merch addresses redirect to epsilonrecords.bandcamp.com, and anything you buy there is handled by Bandcamp under its own privacy policy. The same applies to links to Instagram, SoundCloud, YouTube, Twitch, Mixcloud, TikTok, Facebook, Discord and the streaming and industry services named on our pages. Nothing is sent to those services until you follow a link.

06

Legal bases

We rely on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) to:

  • deliver the website securely and reliably (hosting and DNS);
  • show the sites in their intended typeface (Google Fonts);
  • show the current plugin releases on the plugins site (GitHub);
  • understand, in aggregate and without cookies, how much the sites are used and how fast they load (Cloudflare Web Analytics);
  • answer messages you send us. Where your message is about entering into an agreement with us, such as a release, we also rely on taking steps at your request before a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

We rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR) to send you the newsletter. You give it by entering your address and confirming it in the confirmation email, and you can withdraw it at any time by unsubscribing; that does not affect what we sent before.

You can object to anything we do under legitimate interests at any time (see below).

07

How long data is kept

  • Request data at Netlify, Google Fonts and GitHub: we keep none ourselves; the providers keep it under their own retention practices.
  • Analytics: we only see aggregate reports. Cloudflare does not give us individual-level data.
  • Newsletter: while you are subscribed, your address stays on the subscriber list. After you unsubscribe it stays in Kit, marked as unsubscribed so that you are not emailed again, until you ask us to erase it; we then delete it. Write to the privacy contact to ask.
  • Email: this notice does not specify a fixed retention period for correspondence. Retention depends on the enquiry, any resulting relationship and applicable record-keeping obligations.

08

International transfers

Netlify, Google, Cloudflare and GitHub are based in the United States, and Kit states that it may transfer data there, so data described above may be processed outside the European Economic Area. See Netlify's privacy statement, Google's privacy policy, Cloudflare's privacy policy, GitHub's privacy statement and Kit's privacy policy for their descriptions of international processing.

09

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us for access to the personal data we hold about you, and to correct, erase or restrict it, to receive it in a portable form, or object to our processing it. Write to the privacy contact above. Because the sites themselves collect so little, in practice this mostly concerns your newsletter subscription and emails you have sent us. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In the Netherlands that is the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens; you may also go to the authority where you live or work.

10

Changes

If what the sites collect changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
