A&R Onboarding Guide

Welcome to the Epsilon Records team. As an A&R (Artists and Repertoire) representative you play a crucial role in shaping the future of our label and the careers of our artists. This guide will help you understand your responsibilities and how to navigate your new role successfully.

Onboarding

Ten steps to get started

Step 1

Understanding your role

Talent scouting: Your primary task is to find promising new artists that align with our label's vision and sound. You'll need to stay on top of music trends, attend events, and network within the industry.

Artist development: Work closely with our signed artists to develop their sound, brand, and marketability. Your insight will guide their creative and professional trajectory.

Project oversight: Manage recording projects from inception to release. Ensure that production aligns with our standards and is delivered on schedule.

Weekly review meeting: Join our weekly A&R Music Review Zoom call, every Sunday at 4:00 PM.

Step 2

Familiarize yourself with Epsilon Records

History and vision: Read up on our history, past releases, and future goals. Understanding our legacy is crucial to finding artists that are a good fit.

Current roster: Listen to and get to know our current artists. Appreciating our existing talent will inform your scouting process.

Label culture: Immerse yourself in our label's culture. Engage with the team to understand the Epsilon ethos.

Step 3

Industry networking and resources

Events and conferences: Access our company calendar of upcoming music events, festivals, and conferences. Plan to attend those that are most likely to align with our scouting needs.

Contact database: You'll be given access to our internal contact list of industry professionals. Start building relationships early.

Training resources: Utilize the training materials provided to refine your scouting and artist development skills.

Step 4

Tools and technology

A&R portal: Familiarize yourself with our A&R portal where you'll submit artist finds, update project statuses, and communicate with the team.

Communication platforms: Set up your email, Slack, and other communication tools we use such as WhatsApp. Check these daily.

Reporting systems: Learn how to use our reporting systems for updates on projects and artist development.

Step 5

Legal and administrative set-up

Contracts and agreements: Review sample contracts and understand the terms and conditions we typically offer to artists.

Onboarding paperwork: Complete all necessary onboarding documents, including tax forms and confidentiality agreements.

Compensation structure: Understand how your commission structure works and how to track your earnings.

Step 6

Initial assignments and goals

First artist report: Within your first month, identify and report on at least three potential artists for the label.

Meet the team: Schedule meetings with various department heads to understand how A&R integrates with other parts of the label.

Set personal goals: Establish personal goals for what you aim to achieve in your first quarter with us.

Step 7

Continuous learning and development

Mentorship: You will be paired with a senior A&R rep who will serve as your mentor. Regular check-ins will aid your development.

Workshops and training: Participate in scheduled workshops and training sessions to hone your skills.

Feedback loop: Engage in regular feedback sessions with your music supervisor to ensure continuous improvement.

Step 8

Get involved

Label events: Be present at label events, showcases, and artist signings. These are great opportunities to network and support our artists.

Online presence: Contribute to our online communities and discussions. Your voice should be heard in our forums and social media.

Step 9

Review and reflect

Monthly reviews: Participate in monthly review meetings to discuss your progress, challenges, and strategies moving forward.

Success metrics: Keep track of your successes and learn from any setbacks. Measure your performance against your established goals.

Step 10

Community and culture

Join our community: Actively participate in our community events, online discussions, and culture-building activities.

Epsilon values: Embrace and promote the values and principles of Epsilon Records in all your interactions.

Support

We believe your expertise and passion will be invaluable to our continued success. Dive in, and let's make some noise together.
