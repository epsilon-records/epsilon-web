LabelKit · by Epsilon Records

Your label's catalogue, in one place.

LabelKit is the software Epsilon Records runs on, now open to other labels. It keeps track of releases, recordings, credits and rights, and turns years of records, splits and agreements into a catalogue you can actually search.

02

Credits

Writers, performers, producers and remixers, with their roles, kept accurate release after release.

03

Ownership & agreements

Master and publishing ownership, split sheets and agreements, tracked per recording.

04

Consents & analysis

Consent for AI-adjacent processing, and AudioKit analysis results, recorded against each recording.
