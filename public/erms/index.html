ERMS · Epsilon Records Management System

The label's catalogue, in one place.

ERMS is the system Epsilon Records uses to keep track of its releases, recordings, credits and rights. It turns a decade of records, splits and agreements into a catalogue you can actually search.

What it does

A record for every record

Everything the label needs to know about what it owns, and who made it.

01

Releases & recordings

Each release with its recordings, artwork and track order, from first EP to the latest.

02

Credits

Writers, performers, producers and remixers, with their roles, kept accurate release after release.

03

Ownership & agreements

Master and publishing ownership, split sheets and agreements, tracked per recording.

04

Consents & analysis

Consent for AI-adjacent processing, and AudioKit analysis results, recorded against each recording.
