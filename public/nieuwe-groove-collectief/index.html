Nieuwe Groove Collectief

An Amsterdam-based independent sublabel of Epsilon Records launched in 2024, Nieuwe Groove Collectief epitomizes diversity in music curation. Our collective thrives on a mix of genres, spanning organic electronic, future soul, cinematic electronica, funky house, and nu-disco. We are committed to pushing the boundaries of music production, celebrating diverse and forward-thinking expressions within the music scene.

About the label

A high-vibrational mood

Nieuwe Groove Collectief stands as a beacon for artists and enthusiasts alike, focusing on incorporating unique textures and combining rich, emotive melodies with euphoric elements to create infectious, buttery music and a high-vibrational mood.
